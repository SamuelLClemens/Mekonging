- [x] **2.5** Near-Me drive times CALIBRATED against real routing, and the 90-minute cap raised on evidence rather than deferred again. `scripts/build_drivetimes.py` fits a per-country curve to 16,000+ OSRM-routed journeys between the app's own places (two fetch rounds — a spread sample, then a dense short-range one, because the first had only tens of pairs in the 15–40 km band that "near me" actually depends on). The old model — one flat `km * 1.35 / 50 kmh`, i.e. 1.62 min per straight-line km — measured **23–63% too fast on short journeys and 18–30% too slow on long ones**. Minutes per straight-line km is not constant: it falls with distance, and in Thailand it rises first through the 12–90 km band that is all provincial roads and market towns before the motorways take over.
  Four filters were needed before the fit meant anything, each found by inspecting outliers rather than assumed: a road/straight ratio outside 0.95–2.5 (set to retain Pai → Chiang Mai at 2.35× while excluding barrier detours); an implied average speed outside 25–110 km/h, which is the one that actually catches ferries — Maya Bay → Koh Kradan passes the detour test at 2.23× but implies 18 km/h for eight hours, which is a boat; collapsing place pairs to **corridors** before taking quantiles, because eleven curated places around Krabi share two roads and drowned out the single Bangkok–Ayutthaya motorway pair; and scaling the corridor cell to a quarter of each band's own length, after a fixed ~55 km cell merged both ends of every short pair into one cell and silently made four bands inherit their neighbour's figures.
  Output is p15/p50/p85 per band, not a point. p50 drives near/day-trip classification; the label is now a measured range ("1h 30m–2h 30m by road (est.)") because within a band the spread is real and from two coordinates there is no way to know which road connects them. Scored against six known journeys, 4 of 6 fall inside the range; both misses (Bangkok → Ayutthaya, Phnom Penh → Kampong Cham) are fast trunk roads in regions whose median is much slower, and both were wrong under the old model too. `NEAR_MAX_MIN` 75 → 90: the cap was held down because a straight-line guess put Pai → Chiang Mai at ~89 min, and the calibrated curve puts it at 126, comfortably outside. Near-me radius is now country-specific — 39 km in Thailand, 48 in Laos, 56 in Cambodia, 67 in Vietnam.
